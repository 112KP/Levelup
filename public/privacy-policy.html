Levelup Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy describes how Levelup handles information when you use its web or Android application. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to patel.keyurg37@gmail.com.

Information we handle

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication-provider information, to create and secure your account.
  • Workspace information you add or create, including your display name, job, progress statistics, quests, quest logs, skills, items, dungeons, titles, and activity history.
  • Basic technical information processed by the services needed to deliver the app, authenticate you, and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

Levelup uses account and workspace information to authenticate you and provide, maintain, and protect your workspace. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting for the app. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes information needed to authenticate you. The web app is delivered through GitHub Pages, and the app requests fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

Levelup does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and account deletion

Account and workspace information is retained while your account is active. You can request deletion from the signed-in app or the web deletion page. After you confirm, Levelup deletes the account and associated app records from the active application database. Limited copies may remain temporarily in service backups or security records under the relevant providers' retention practices. Deleting your Levelup account does not delete your Google account.

Visit Delete your Levelup account to start a request. If you cannot sign in, email patel.keyurg37@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to or correction or deletion of personal information. Contact us at patel.keyurg37@gmail.com with questions or requests. We may need to verify that you control the account before responding.

Security and your choices

We use Supabase authentication and access controls to protect account data. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your sign-in credentials private and contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its services change. The effective date above indicates when it was most recently updated.
