Delete your Levelup account

You can request deletion in the Levelup app or on the web. Sign in to the account you want removed, open the account menu, choose Delete account, and confirm by typing DELETE.

When a deletion request completes successfully, Levelup deletes your authentication account and associated profile, quests, quest history, activity history, skills, items, titles, and dungeons from the active application database. This action cannot be undone. It does not delete your Google account.

If you cannot sign in or complete the request, contact patel.keyurg37@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Levelup account. We may ask you to verify account ownership.
